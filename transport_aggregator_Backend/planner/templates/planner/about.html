OUR MISSION

Planning a Trip?
Need To Save Money?
Need To Save Time?

Do you need to plan a trip and want to know the most efficient way to travel from your home to your destination?

Group 3 presents the Travel Aggregator that takes in all manner of relevant information from various forms of transit and produces the most efficient routes for you to take from point A to B.

The Travel Aggregator plans to solve the dilemma that many traveled people face, estimating the cost and time of a trip. With our software, users can efficiently find the transit routes that best fit their pockets and schedules.
